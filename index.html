II · La vostra identità

Angelina Ravelli

You are an eccentric heiress and tireless traveller whose adventures, opinions and stories have made you a memorable guest across Europe. You have an excellent memory for unusual places, objects and people—even if your storytelling occasionally takes the scenic route.

Play Angelina as lively, curious and slightly unpredictable, but not foolish. You may seem whimsical, yet you notice visual details and remember odd things precisely when they catch your attention.

Perché siete qui

Clarissa invited you because you are an old-world social fixture with stories from everywhere and because your travels have brought you into contact with collectors and rare wines far beyond Tuscany.

Come vestirsi

Colourful, elegant late-1960s heiress abroad: a chic dress or ensemble with one memorable accessory, scarf, jewellery or travel-inspired detail. Sophisticated, individual and slightly eccentric rather than comic.

IV · In confidenza

Da leggere con discrezione

During a visit to Geneva, you once saw a bottle being presented privately as Antonelli–Mastropietro Riserva 1947. Something about the label struck you as slightly different from Antonelli material you later saw. You mentioned the sighting to Patrizio, and he took it far more seriously than you expected. You subsequently sent him a postcard with the details.

The bottle in Geneva was not being shown by the estate. You remember hearing that it had passed through the hands of a French collector, but you were never given a name. Do not turn that vague memory into certainty. What matters is that another supposed 1947 existed outside the estate and Patrizio considered its appearance significant.

Da ricordare

If someone asks about other 1947 bottles or foreign sightings, tell the Geneva story and share the information from your postcard. Be precise about what you know: you heard “a French collector,” not a specific person.

V · Prima di cena

Durante l’aperitivo

I

Tell Patrizio that you still remember the Geneva bottle and ask why the detail interested him so much.

II

Ask Fabrice whether exceptionally rare bottles often pass quietly through private collectors before appearing at auction.

III

Ask Clarissa whether the estate ever released or sold bottles of the 1947 vintage abroad in later years.

IV

Before becoming too serious, tell at least one entertaining travel story—then use the conversation to find out who seems particularly interested in where rare bottles have travelled.
